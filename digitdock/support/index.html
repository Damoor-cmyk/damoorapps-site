DigitDock Support

Turn on the keyboard

  1. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards.
  2. Tap Add New Keyboard… and choose DigitDock.
  3. In any app, tap the 🌐 key (or hold it) to switch to DigitDock.

You don't need to turn on "Allow Full Access". DigitDock works fully without it.

Common questions

Why does DigitDock disappear in some fields?
In password fields and phone-number keypads, your phone switches to its own keyboard for security. Some apps, such as many banking apps, block all third-party keyboards. DigitDock hasn't crashed. It comes back as soon as you tap a normal text field.

I changed my theme, Quick Phrases, or Auto-Correction, but the keyboard didn't change.
Changes reach the keyboard the next time it opens. Close the keyboard (tap outside the text field) and open it again.

How do I get a click sound on each key?
Go to Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Keyboard Feedback and turn on Sound.

How do I add a Quick Phrase?
Open the DigitDock app. Type a phrase and tap Add, or copy text anywhere and tap the Paste button. Then tap 📋 on the keyboard to insert it.

How do I move the cursor?
Hold the space bar for a moment, then slide left or right.

Does DigitDock see what I type?
No. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.
