DigitDock Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

In short: DigitDock collects no data. Nothing you type leaves your phone, and we don't collect, store, sell, or share anything about you.

Who we are

DigitDock is a keyboard app made by Damoor Apps. This policy covers the DigitDock app and the DigitDock keyboard.

What you type

  • DigitDock never asks for "Full Access". Without Full Access, iOS does not let a keyboard use the internet, so the keyboard cannot send anything anywhere.
  • The keyboard does not save, log, or learn what you type. Word suggestions and auto-correct are worked out on your phone from the word you are typing right now, and then forgotten.
  • There is no personal dictionary and no typing history.

What is saved on your phone

A few things are saved, only on your phone, so the app and keyboard work the way you set them:

  • Your theme, the Auto-Correction setting, and your Quick Phrases. These are saved in storage that the DigitDock app and the DigitDock keyboard share on your device. Only the app changes them. The keyboard only reads them.
  • Recently used emoji (up to 30). These are kept inside the keyboard's own storage. Not even the DigitDock app can see them.

We never receive any of this. Your phone may include app data in your own device backups, which you control through your phone's backup settings.

Clipboard

The keyboard never reads your clipboard. The DigitDock app reads it only when you tap the Paste button to save a Quick Phrase, and only that one time.

What DigitDock does not do

  • No internet connection, in the app or the keyboard.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera.

Deleting your data

You can delete Quick Phrases in the app at any time. Deleting DigitDock from your phone removes everything it saved.

Children

DigitDock collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
